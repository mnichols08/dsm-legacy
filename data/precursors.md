The Mitsubishi engines that directly preceded the 4G63, both in racing and production, were mainly from the Sirius engine family and earlier turbocharged four-cylinder engines:

### Key Predecessor Engines

- **4G61 Turbo (Sirius family)**: This 1.6L turbocharged engine powered vehicles like the Mitsubishi Colt Turbo and was a direct architectural sibling in the Sirius family leading to the 4G63's design.[1]
- **4G63T introduction in the early 1980s**: The first turbocharged 4G63 engine was introduced in the 1980 Mitsubishi Lancer EX 2000 Turbo, which was a racing and performance vehicle setting the stage for later rally versions. The 4G63 found racing success powering the Galant VR-4 and evolved for the Lancer Evolution lineup.[2][1]
- **Mitsubishi Starion engines**: The Starion ran a turbocharged inline-4, adopting many technologies such as turbocharging and durable block design, which influenced the 4G63's development for high-performance turbocharged applications.[1][2]

### Racing and Production Influence

- **1980s Group A Rally Cars**: The 4G63 turbo powered Mitsubishi's group A rally cars like the Galant VR-4 and later the Lancer Evolution series, which solidified its reputation for performance and durability in motorsport.[3][2]
- **Astron and Saturn engine families**: While not direct predecessors, these earlier engine families provided mechanical and design concepts, especially in balance and turbocharging technology, which influenced the refinement of the 4G63.[4]

***

In summary, the 4G63 was directly preceded by the turbocharged variants of the Sirius series, such as the 4G61T and the initial 4G63T engines from the early 1980s Lancer and Starion models. These engines were the foundation for the championship-winning rally engines and production DSM vehicles that made the 4G63 famous.[2][3][1]
